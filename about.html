About Me

Hi, I'm Mihir — a B.Tech IT student at MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune. I’m passionate about technology, creativity, and solving real‑world problems through code.

My journey began with curiosity — experimenting with small projects and learning how software can make life smarter. Over time, I’ve developed skills in web development, IoT, and cloud computing.

I enjoy working on projects that challenge me to think differently and push boundaries. Whether it’s designing a clean web interface or integrating sensors with microcontrollers, I’m always excited to learn something new.

Outside of coding, I’m a cricket enthusiast and love discovering new gadgets. I believe creativity and technology go hand in hand — and every project is a chance to grow and innovate.
